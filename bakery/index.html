Questions

Asked at the counter, answered here.

Is it all maida-free?

The country, rye, and sandwich loaves use atta with no added maida. The pav and banana bread do contain maida — about 30% — because neither works properly without it. The shelf tags say so, and the staff will tell you the same.

How do I store sourdough in Pune’s humidity?

Cloth bag on the counter for two days, then slice everything, freeze in a dabba, and toast straight from frozen. Don’t refrigerate the whole loaf; it goes stale faster in the fridge.

Why are you closed on Mondays?

Two reasons. The starter gets a rest day, and so does the one person who does the baking. Everything else in this shop follows from that.

How do I pay?

UPI or cash at pickup. We don’t take cards — the machine rental cost more than the machine processed — and we don’t take advance payment for subscriptions.

Allergies?

Everything is baked in the same small room, so assume traces of wheat, sesame, and milk everywhere. If you have coeliac disease, this is not your bakery, and we’d rather say so than sell you something unsafe.

Do you do birthday cakes or bulk orders?

No cakes, and no wedding orders. For office orders above ten loaves with two days’ notice, call and we’ll tell you honestly whether the oven schedule allows it that week.
